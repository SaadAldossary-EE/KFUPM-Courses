# 🚁 UAV Localization Systems for Avalanche Rescue

## 📌 Overview

This project was completed for **ENGL 214: Academic and Professional Communication** at King Fahd University of Petroleum & Minerals (KFUPM).

The project investigates victim-location delays during avalanche rescue operations at Colorado ski resorts and evaluates how **Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) localization systems** could reduce the time required to locate injured or buried skiers.

---

## ⚠️ Problem

Avalanche rescue teams may face significant delays when locating victims due to:

- 📍 Imprecise victim-location information from emergency calls and cellular tower pings
- 📡 The need for physical debris sweeping when using RECCO detection systems
- 🌡️ The inability of thermal imaging systems to detect victims buried beneath deep snow

These limitations can delay rescue teams from reaching victims during critical rescue periods.

---

## 🚁 UAV Localization Technologies Evaluated

### 📱 1. Cellular Signal Localization

UAVs equipped with cellular communication systems can detect signals from a victim's mobile phone and combine signal information with UAV GPS coordinates to estimate the victim's location.

Research evaluated in the report demonstrated localization of mobile phones in approximately **3 minutes**.

### 📡 2. UAV-Mounted Radar

A downward-facing radar mounted on a UAV can scan avalanche debris and estimate the location of buried victims before rescuers begin extensive ground searches.

Experimental research demonstrated detection of buried targets at depths of up to **40 cm beneath snow**.

### 📶 3. Wireless Signal Detection

UAVs equipped with wireless receivers can detect signals transmitted by avalanche transceivers carried by buried victims.

This approach allows rescue teams to identify potential victim locations without performing large manual search sweeps.

---

## 🔋 Engineering Challenge

The project also evaluates **UAV battery endurance in cold environments**.

Low temperatures can reduce lithium-ion battery discharge capacity and UAV flight endurance. Automated battery-swapping and battery-management systems were examined as potential methods for maintaining continuous UAV search operations.

---

## 📊 Key Findings

| Technology | Purpose |
|---|---|
| 📱 Cellular Signal Localization | Locate victims through mobile-phone signals |
| 📡 UAV-Mounted Radar | Detect potential victims beneath avalanche debris |
| 📶 Wireless Signal Detection | Locate buried avalanche transceivers |
| 🔋 Battery Management | Improve UAV endurance in cold environments |

---

## 🎯 Conclusion

The study found that UAV-based localization technologies have the potential to reduce victim-location delays by providing rescue teams with more precise location information before extensive ground searching begins.

The evaluated technologies include:

- **Cellular signal localization**
- **UAV-mounted radar**
- **Avalanche-transceiver signal detection**

While battery endurance remains an operational challenge, battery-management technologies could improve the practicality of UAV localization systems in cold mountain environments.
