### 🚁 ENGL 214 — Academic & Professional Communication

#### UAV Localization Systems for Avalanche Rescue

An engineering research project investigating **victim-location delays during avalanche rescue operations at Colorado ski resorts** and evaluating UAV-based technologies that could improve victim localization.

**Technologies evaluated:**
- 📱 Cellular signal localization
- 📡 UAV-mounted radar
- 📶 Avalanche-transceiver signal detection
- 🔋 UAV battery management for cold environments

The project combined technical research, engineering analysis, and professional communication to evaluate the feasibility and limitations of UAV-assisted rescue systems.

**Areas:**  
`UAV Systems` • `Wireless Localization` • `Radar Systems` • `Engineering Research`

### 🧺 BUS 200 — Business & Entrepreneurship

#### Lumah — Laundry Marketplace & Delivery Platform

A startup concept for a digital marketplace connecting customers with local laundries across **Dammam, Khobar, and Dhahran**.

The project developed the concept from problem identification through market analysis, business modeling, and financial feasibility.

**Key work:**
- 📊 TAM / SAM / SOM market analysis
- 🏆 Competitive analysis
- 💎 Value Proposition Canvas
- 🧩 Business Model Canvas
- 💰 Revenue and pricing model
- 📈 Unit economics and break-even analysis
- 💸 Funding strategy

The Year-1 financial model projected **12,000 orders**, **SAR 220,807 in platform revenue**, and operational break-even at approximately **11,104 orders per year**.

**Areas:**  
`Entrepreneurship` • `Business Modeling` • `Financial Modeling` • `Market Analysis` • `Product Strategy`
