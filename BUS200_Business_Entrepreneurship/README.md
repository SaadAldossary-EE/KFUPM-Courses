# 🧺 Lumah — Laundry Marketplace & Delivery Platform

## 📌 Overview

**Lumah** is a startup concept developed for **BUS 200: Business & Entrepreneurship** at King Fahd University of Petroleum & Minerals (KFUPM).

Lumah is a digital marketplace designed to connect customers with local laundries through a single platform for **laundry discovery, price comparison, pickup scheduling, real-time order tracking, and doorstep delivery**.

The initial launch market focuses on **Dammam, Khobar, and Dhahran** in Saudi Arabia's Eastern Province.

---

## ⚠️ The Problem

Customers looking for laundry services may face several challenges:

- 🔍 Difficulty finding and comparing trusted local laundries
- 🚗 Multiple trips for laundry drop-off and pickup
- 📍 Limited visibility into order status
- ⭐ Difficulty comparing prices, services, and customer reviews
- ⏱️ Time lost coordinating laundry services

---

## 💡 The Solution

Lumah brings multiple local laundries into one marketplace where customers can:

- 🧺 Discover and compare nearby laundries
- ⭐ View ratings and reviews
- 💰 Compare prices and services
- 🚚 Schedule pickup and delivery
- 📍 Track orders in real time
- 💳 Pay through the platform
- 🎟️ Access membership plans and offers

---

## 📱 How It Works

### 1️⃣ Choose a Laundry
Browse and compare trusted local laundries based on price, ratings, and services.

### 2️⃣ Payment
Review the order and complete payment through the platform.

### 3️⃣ Track Your Order
Follow the order from pickup through the laundry process to delivery.

### 4️⃣ Delivered
Clean clothes are delivered directly to the customer's door.

---

## 💼 Business Model

Lumah follows an **asset-light marketplace model**.

The platform does not own laundries, delivery vehicles, or drivers. Instead, it connects customers, existing laundries, and third-party delivery partners.

### 💰 Revenue Streams

- 🧺 Laundry transaction commissions
- 🚚 Delivery commissions
- 🎟️ Customer memberships
- 📢 Advertising and sponsored listings

This structure allows the platform to scale without directly operating laundry facilities or maintaining a delivery fleet.

## 🏆 Competitive Advantage

Unlike single-brand laundry applications, Lumah is designed as a **marketplace connecting customers with multiple laundries**.

### For Customers
- 🔎 More choice
- 💰 Transparent pricing
- ⭐ Ratings and reviews
- 🚚 Flexible pickup and delivery
- 📍 Real-time order tracking

### For Laundries
- 👥 Access to additional customers
- 📱 Digital marketplace presence
- 💸 No upfront platform joining fee
- 📈 Additional sales through the platform

## 💸 Funding Strategy

The project estimated an initial funding requirement of **SAR 250,000** to cover:

- 📱 Platform development and launch costs
- 📢 Initial marketing
- 🤝 Laundry partner onboarding
- 💼 Working capital
- 🛡️ Cash reserve

The financial analysis considered **founder/family capital, accelerator or incubator funding, and angel investment**, with non-dilutive funding and founder capital identified as the preferred initial approach.
